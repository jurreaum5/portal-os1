# Institute Policies

## Purpose

This registry defines the adopted policy set for MAX Institute, USS, ICVS, and Portal-OS1 operations.

## Policy registry

### POL-001 Research Policy
Purpose:
- govern research integrity, evidence handling, and review discipline

Requirements:
- traceable evidence and source records
- review and reproducibility controls
- explicit treatment of uncertainty

Workflow:
- define hypothesis
- collect evidence
- validate lineage
- review against policy and standards

Approval Rules:
- research activity requires documented review before production use

Audit Rules:
- all research findings must be retained with evidence lineage and review context

### POL-002 Publication Policy
Purpose:
- govern publication readiness, disclosure, and communication standards

Requirements:
- verification before release
- clear disclosure of assumptions and evidence limits
- review for governance and compliance alignment

Workflow:
- draft evidence package
- validate approval state
- confirm publication readiness
- publish with lineage metadata

Approval Rules:
- publication requires explicit approval from the designated governance authority

Audit Rules:
- publication history must be retained and reviewable

### POL-003 Standards Policy
Purpose:
- define the standards base for architecture, code, and operational practice

Requirements:
- explicit standards adoption
- implementation consistency
- evidence-backed compliance

Workflow:
- define applicable standard
- validate implementation against standard
- record deviations and approvals

Approval Rules:
- standard exceptions require approved rationale and traceable sign-off

Audit Rules:
- standards compliance must be retained in audit logs

### POL-004 Protocol Policy
Purpose:
- define protocol validity, compatibility, and versioning expectations

Requirements:
- protocol version control
- compatibility checks
- failure and rollback documentation

Workflow:
- confirm protocol scope
- validate compatibility
- record state and approvals

Approval Rules:
- protocol changes require governance-level review before activation

Audit Rules:
- protocol history must remain available for review and recovery

### POL-005 Simulation Policy
Purpose:
- govern simulation validity, confidence, and operational use

Requirements:
- model registration
- replay support
- provenance and confidence controls

Workflow:
- register model
- run with versioned inputs
- review output confidence
- approve or reject for operational use

Approval Rules:
- operational use of a simulation requires explicit approval and validation

Audit Rules:
- simulation records must retain assumptions, versioning, and outcome lineage

### POL-006 Identity Policy
Purpose:
- govern identity, continuity, access boundaries, and traceability

Requirements:
- explicit identity ownership
- access boundary controls
- continuity and recovery safeguards

Workflow:
- establish identity record
- validate permissions and lineage
- maintain continuity checks

Approval Rules:
- identity changes require governance review and permission validation

Audit Rules:
- identity history and access events must be retained for review

### POL-007 Governance Policy
Purpose:
- regulate authority, reviews, approvals, and operational governance

Requirements:
- defined authority bodies
- approval thresholds and escalation paths
- documented exceptions and auditability

Workflow:
- identify governance need
- route for review
- record decision and rationale
- enforce final decision

Approval Rules:
- governance decisions require a recorded source of authority and rationale

Audit Rules:
- governance decisions must be retained, reviewable, and recoverable

### POL-008 AI Policy
Purpose:
- govern AI model use, safety, provenance, and operational boundaries

Requirements:
- explicit model selection and approval
- no untracked use of external inference
- lineage and confidence controls

Workflow:
- select provider and model
- confirm policy fit
- validate outputs and confidence
- maintain evidence trail

Approval Rules:
- production AI use requires governance approval and operational review

Audit Rules:
- AI usage and output records must be retained for review and recovery

### POL-009 Provider Policy
Purpose:
- govern production provider operations, failover, and dependencies

Requirements:
- provider state checks
- failover readiness
- operational monitoring and cost transparency

Workflow:
- register provider
- validate health and capabilities
- monitor performance
- enforce failover when degraded or blocked

Approval Rules:
- provider activation requires sign-off from relevant operational authority

Audit Rules:
- provider health, failures, and fallback events must be retained

### POL-010 Recovery Policy
Purpose:
- define failure response, rollback, and continuity obligations

Requirements:
- documented recovery plan
- rollback controls
- evidence of continuity restoration

Workflow:
- detect issue
- classify severity
- activate fallback or rollback
- verify recovery and record outcome

Approval Rules:
- recovery plans and rollback actions must be documented and approved as required

Audit Rules:
- recovery actions and outcomes must be stored for audit and review

## Governance summary

All policies share the same mandatory structure:

- Purpose
- Requirements
- Workflow
- Approval Rules
- Audit Rules

This ensures consistent enforcement and traceability across Institute, USS, ICVS, and Portal-OS1 operations.
